# D73: design interview

Decision: docs/decisions.md D73 (commit f206b5e). The first step of a D72 design session (#164).

- Ask 2–3 short questions (who looks at it and when, what it should feel like).
- Spread about nine references over three deliberately different directions; mix neighbouring fields (games, print, objects, pixel art) and stretch axes (cute–serious, dense–airy, colourful–mono). Web: collections such as Mobbin/Dribbble; terminal UIs: well-made TUIs (lazygit, btop). Show them with the tool chosen per D72 addendum.
- The person only picks likes and dislikes; dislikes are information too.
- Extract shared principles in words; then 2–3 drafts, each tied to a reference.
- Do not copy: draw new art. Never commit reference images; keep links and notes of what was referenced (images stay local).
- Keep: reference links and notes, the principles (design standard), the chosen draft in docs/design/<topic>/.
