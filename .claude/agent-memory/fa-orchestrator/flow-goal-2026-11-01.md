FA's top goal (Jin via PM, 2026-11-01): issue-level work as parallel and fast as resources allow. When reached, viral launch starts; the rest waits for contributors.
Done: (1) flow efficiency ≥ 70% = work minutes (implementing, baseline, checking, reviewing, ci, merging, resolving-conflict) ÷ (approval→merge minutes − machine-resource waits: test-slot-wait, executor/limit-wait) — formula changed by Jin 11-01; FA-made waits (merge turn, overlap, queued/held gaps) stay in, over issues merged in the last 24h; (2) near-zero minutes/day with startable work and a free machine slot; (3) no reverted merges; (4) holds a day with no human hands.

**Why:** Jin wants launch gated on throughput, not completeness.
