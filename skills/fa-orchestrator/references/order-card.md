# Order draft and card

Keep one durable order draft in `docs/specs/` and use the project's decision log for settled
context. Use the repository's `fa-order` skill to clarify only unresolved product choices, split work
into independently verifiable issues and prepare a plan JSON.

The card lists the new issues grouped by lane, their dependencies, Done when checks, and any new
lanes (human name, paths, skills).

Retain compact pending decisions, attempts and draft references across turns; re-read current facts
when handling the next event.
