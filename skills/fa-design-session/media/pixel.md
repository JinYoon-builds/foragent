# Pixel art research pack

## References

- Lospec: palettes and pixel art tutorials.
- Pixel art from games of the same size and mood; look at outlines and shading.
- Neighbouring fields: cross-stitch patterns, mosaic tiles, LED signs.

Save the link and a short note per reference. Learn the technique; do not copy
the art.

## Pitfalls

- Half-block resolution: in a terminal each cell holds two vertical pixels
  (`▀`/`▄`), so plan the sprite size in cells and check the aspect ratio.
- Dark outlines vanish on dark backgrounds: use a dark brown instead of black.
- Keep a small, fixed palette and test it on light and dark terminals.
