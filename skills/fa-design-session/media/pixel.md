# Pixel art research pack

## References

Explore [Lospec palettes](https://lospec.com/palette-list) and pixel art examples
for silhouette, palette size, outline treatment and animation rhythm. Compare
directions from games, small physical toys and printed icons as well as sprites.
Return original links and notes about the qualities to explore. Draw original
art; reference images remain local and are never committed.

## Pitfalls

- Establish the actual pixel grid and display scale before drawing. For terminal
  half-block rendering, each cell carries an upper and lower pixel; compare at
  the resulting resolution and terminal cell proportions.
- Inspect silhouettes at native size. Avoid smoothing or scaling that hides
  unreadable details in the preview.
- Black outlines can disappear on dark backgrounds. Try dark brown or another
  palette colour and inspect contrast against the actual background.
- Compare animation poses and state changes, including idle and completion,
  so motion and meaning remain readable at the chosen size.

For terminal art, also open [the terminal UI pack](tui.md) in this same `media/`
folder. Record the grid, palette, background and rendering method with the chosen
draft.
