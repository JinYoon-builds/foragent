# Mobile research pack

## References

- Mobbin: iOS and Android screens and flows by pattern.
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Material Design: platform conventions.
- Neighbouring fields: watch faces, car displays, physical controls.

Save the link and a short note per reference. Do not commit screenshots.

## Pitfalls

- Platform conventions: back navigation, tab bars, sheets and system gestures
  differ between iOS and Android.
- Touch size: targets at least 44pt (iOS) / 48dp (Android); keep them apart.
- Safe areas, notches, the on-screen keyboard and one-handed reach.
- Dynamic type and dark mode.
